# 10 — Script Writing SOP

> Permanent internal documentation. Senior Health YouTube Production System.
> This document is standalone and self-contained.

---

## 1. Full Script Structure

| Section | Timing | Function |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Hook | 0–60s | Five-beat architecture (see `05_Hook_Blueprints.md`) |
| Stakes | 60–120s | Why this matters to the viewer's life |
| Credibility | 120–180s | Presenter identity + one cited study |
| Countdown Body | Bulk | Reveal items in ascending importance |
| Micro-loops | Between items | "But #2 is even more important…" |
| Recap + CTA | Final | Restate strongest payoff, soft subscribe |

---

## 2. Section State Machine

| State | Time | Exit Condition | Next |
|-------|------|----------------|------|
| HOOK | 0–60s | Open loop set | STAKES |
| STAKES | 60–120s | Personal relevance established | CREDIBILITY |
| CREDIBILITY | 120–180s | Presenter and study cited | BODY |
| BODY | Bulk | All items revealed, ascending | RECAP |
| RECAP | Final | Strongest payoff restated + CTA | END |

---

## 3. Invariants

| ID | Rule | Severity |
|----|------|----------|
| I1 | Loop opened every 60–90s | Block |
| I2 | Items revealed in ascending importance | Block |
| I3 | Strongest item last and pre-teased | Warn |
| I4 | At least one actionable takeaway | Block |
| I5 | Payoff matches title | Block |
| I6 | Clinician caveat present | Block |

---

## 4. Transitions

Bridge each section with a re-teased loop. Never close a loop without opening the next. Use verbal signposts ("before I show you #1, you need to understand this").

---

## 5. Retention Methods

1. Short sentences; sensory language.
2. Re-open a loop every 60–90 seconds.
3. Save the strongest item for last and re-tease it early and often.
4. Use micro-cliffhangers between sections.

---

## 6. Viewer Engagement

- Direct second-person address
- Rhetorical questions
- Physical participation prompts
- Symptom checklists that invite self-recognition

---

## 7. Value-Add Framework

Every video must deliver at least one genuinely useful, evidence-backed, actionable takeaway that justifies the runtime and the promise. The payoff must match the title (invariant I5). Bait-and-switch is prohibited and blocked at QA.

---

## 8. Quality Checklist

- [ ] Hook follows the five-beat structure
- [ ] Every claim traces to a cited source
- [ ] One clear actionable takeaway present
- [ ] Payoff matches the title promise
- [ ] Clinician caveat included
- [ ] Loops maintained throughout (every 60–90s)
- [ ] Strongest item revealed last
