# 02 — Brand Guidelines

> Permanent internal documentation. Senior Health YouTube Production System.
> This document is standalone and self-contained.

---

## 1. Brand Voice

Warm, authoritative, and protective. The channel speaks as a knowledgeable ally who respects the viewer's intelligence and life experience while delivering information they can act on. Never condescending, never alarmist beyond what evidence supports.

**Voice vector (0–10 scale):**

| Attribute | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| Warmth | 9 |
| Authority | 8 |
| Hype | 2 |
| Condescension | 0 |
| Directness | 9 |

---

## 2. Tone

Direct and personal (second person, "you"). Calm confidence, not hype. Fear is used only to signal genuine stakes, never to manufacture panic beyond the facts. Relief and empowerment are the dominant emotional destinations.

---

## 3. Writing Style Rules

1. Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
2. Second-person address throughout.
3. Concrete and sensory over abstract (e.g., "squint at the menu across the restaurant," not "vision problems").
4. Specific over vague (named nutrients, exact numbers, precise body parts).
5. No jargon without immediate plain-language translation.

---

## 4. Trust Signals

| Signal | Application | Required |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| Consistent presenter | Same recognizable identity across videos | Yes |
| Cited research | One named study or body per video, accurately represented | Yes |
| Balanced framing | Acknowledge nuance; avoid absolute cures | Yes |
| Appropriate caveats | In-video reminder to consult a clinician | Yes |
| Specificity | Named items, exact figures, precise anatomy | Yes |

---

## 5. Medical Positioning

The channel explains mechanisms and evidence in accessible language. It never diagnoses individuals, never promises cures, and never instructs viewers to stop or alter prescribed medication without a clinician. All claims must be defensible against the evidence standards defined in `09_Medical_Research_SOP.md`.

**Positioning flags:**

| Flag | Setting |
|------|---------|
| Allow individual diagnosis | No |
| Allow cure promise | No |
| Allow medication-alteration instruction | No |
| Require clinician caveat | Yes |

---

## 6. Viewer Experience

Every video should leave the viewer feeling more capable, less anxious, and equipped with one clear, low-effort action.

**Emotional arc sequence:**

`recognition → concern → understanding → relief → empowerment`
