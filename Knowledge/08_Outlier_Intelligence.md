# 08 — Outlier Intelligence

> Permanent internal documentation. Senior Health YouTube Production System.
> This document is standalone and self-contained.

---

## 1. Core Competitive Pattern

The niche herds around diabetes-food and blood-pressure content, over-saturating those lanes. This raises competition there and leaves vision, feet, sleep, specific nutrients, liver, balance, and prevention-age content comparatively open — the same audience and the same fears, but with far less supply.

---

## 2. Proven Winning Formulas (with evidence)

| Formula | Peak Result | Note |
|---------|-------------|------|
| Instant curiosity loop (low fear) | 1,300,000 | Highest ceiling; most scalable |
| Named-drug insider | 892,000 | Barely used beyond BP drugs |
| Ranked authority list on underserved topic | 721,000 (vision) | Under-competed topic advantage |
| Effortless-miracle + independence | 382,000 | Aspirational |
| Body-part diagnostic | 231,000 | Fatigues on repetition (clone fell to 81,000) |

---

## 3. Key Findings

1. **CTR ≠ psychological score.** The top performer (clove/coffee) ranked mid-pack psychologically but won on curiosity + novelty + low fear, indicating a higher ceiling for low-fear content.
2. **Under-competed high-fear topics win with low title effort.** The vision video ranked near the bottom psychologically yet placed #2 by views because demand was strong and supply was thin.
3. **Diagnostic formats fatigue.** The nails clone dropped from 231,000 to 81,000, proving body-part rotation is required.
4. **Relief beats fear for reach.** Forbidden-food reprieve titles (ice cream, chocolate, cheese) outperformed generic diabetes lists.

## 6. Emerging Trends & Levers

1. **Social proof** ("millions of seniors," results counts) is absent across all analyzed competitors — a high-value untapped lever.
2. **Low-fear curiosity content** shows the highest performance ceiling.
3. **Diagnostic rotation** is required to counter format fatigue.
4. **Prevention-age brackets** (50s, 80s) expand the addressable audience with almost no competition.
