## 3. Viewer Objections

| Objection | Neutralizing Technique |
|-----------|------------------------|
| "This is just clickbait" | Deliver a real, specific payoff early |
| "My doctor never said this" | Frame as what rushed appointments omit, not anti-medicine |
| "Too good to be true" | Cite a study; avoid implausible numbers |
| "This doesn't apply to me" | Apply a self-identification trigger (age, named drug, symptom) |

## 5. Curiosity Triggers

- Capitalized "THIS" as a mystery placeholder
- "What happens…"
- "Hidden…"
- Withheld number ("11 truths")
- Body-as-oracle ("your nails record your heart")
- Fast-payoff tease ("10 minutes later")

---

## 6. Trust Triggers

- Consistent presenter identity
- One cited study
- Concrete mechanism explanation
- Balanced framing
- Clinician caveat
- Specificity

---

## 7. Decision Psychology

| Decision | Made In | Driven By |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| Click | < 2 seconds | Self-identification + emotional anchor + curiosity gap |
| Watch (renewed) | Every 60–90 seconds | Open loops |
| Trust (subscribe/return) | End of video | Whether payoff matched promise |

---

## 8. Retention Psychology

1. Open a new loop before closing the previous one.
2. Re-tease the strongest item repeatedly.
3. Reveal list items in ascending importance.
4. Use micro-cliffhangers between sections.

---

## 9. CTR Psychology

CTR is won by four atoms, all legible at mobile thumbnail size:

- One dominant readable word
- One emotional face
- One recognizable hero object
- One unresolved question
