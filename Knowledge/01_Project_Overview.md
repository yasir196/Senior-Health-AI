# 01 — Project Overview

> Permanent internal documentation. Senior Health YouTube Production System.
> This document is standalone and self-contained.

---

## 1. Mission

To build the world's most trusted and highest-performing Senior Health YouTube channel by pairing algorithm-optimized packaging with medically defensible content, serving adults aged 50+ with information that protects their independence, health, and peace of mind.

---

## 2. Goals

1. Achieve category leadership in the Senior Health niche through consistent, format-disciplined production.
2. Win by intersection: apply proven content formats to underserved topics rather than imitating saturated competitors.
3. Build a compounding trust asset with a repeat 60+ audience, treating accuracy as a growth driver, not a constraint.
4. Systematize production so every video is engineered from blueprints rather than improvised.

---

## 3. Channel Strategy

The channel operates on a single master formula:

**Proven Format × Underserved Topic × (Fear OR Relief anchor) × Withheld Payoff × One Hero Object.**

Three proven formats — the body-part diagnostic, the named-drug insider, and the hidden-mistake list — are pointed at whitespace topics (vision, feet, sleep, specific nutrients, balance, early cognitive decline) where audience fear is high and competitor supply is low.

---

## 4. Brand Positioning

The channel is positioned as the trusted insider ally for older adults — an authority that explains what rushed clinical appointments leave out, without crossing into misinformation. It occupies the space between clickable and credible, deliberately avoiding both dry medical content (low CTR) and unsupported sensationalism (policy risk, trust erosion).

---

## 5. Target Audience

| Segment | Age | Traits | Priority |
|---------|-----|--------|----------|
| Primary | 60–80 | Health-anxious, chronic condition, distrust rushed doctors, independence-motivated | 1 |
| Prevention | 50–59 | Proactive, early-symptom curious | 2 |
| Caregiver | Any | Researching for a parent | 2 |
| Elder | 80+ | Independence preservation | 3 |

---

## 6. Success Metrics

| Metric | Validates | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Click-through rate (CTR) | Packaging | ≥ 0.06 |
| Average view duration (%) | Hook + script | ≥ 0.45 |
| Returning viewer rate | Trust asset | ≥ 0.30 |
| Views-per-hour (first 48h) | Outlier potential | Benchmark vs. channel median |
| Watch time per topic cluster | Portfolio allocation | Trend upward |
| Policy / monetization status | Channel survival | Green |

---

## 7. Governing Principles

1. Format discipline over improvisation.
2. Whitespace over imitation.
3. Rotate emotional anchors (≈40% fear / 40% relief / 20% curiosity-only) to prevent fear fatigue.
4. Accuracy is non-negotiable and treated as a growth asset.
