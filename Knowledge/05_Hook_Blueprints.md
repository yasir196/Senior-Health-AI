# 05 — Hook Blueprints

> Permanent internal documentation. Senior Health YouTube Production System.
> This document is standalone and self-contained.

---

## 1. The Five-Beat Hook Architecture (0–60s)

| Beat | Time | Function | Required |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| 1 | 0–5s | Pattern interrupt (command / shocking claim / story cold-open) | Yes |
| 2 | 5–15s | Self-identification (symptom / age / drug / scenario) | Yes |
| 3 | 15–30s | Emotional anchor (fear "nobody told them" OR relief "I owe you an apology") | Yes |
| 4 | 30–45s | Credibility injection (presenter ID + one hard statistic) | Yes |
| 5 | 45–60s | Open loop + numbered promise | Yes |

---

## 2. Opening Strategies (ranked)

| ID | Strategy | Example | Rank |
|----|----------|---------|------|
| O1 | Physical participation | "Hold up your hand and look right now" | 1 |
| O2 | Anti-authority secret | "Your doctor will never tell you this" | 2 |
| O3 | Symptom checklist self-ID | "If you have even one of these four signs" | 3 |
| O4 | Sensory scenario | "You squint at the menu and everything goes fuzzy" | 4 |
| O5 | Narrative cold-open | "Picture this: a 71-year-old man, still mowing his lawn…" | 5 |
| O6 | Contrarian belief attack | "If you think walking is the best exercise, listen closely" | 6 |

---

## 3. Curiosity Loop Techniques

- Withheld payoff tease ("six words that change everything")
- Numbered promise ("11 truths")
- Countdown structure ("6 additions, saving the strongest for last")
- Body-as-oracle claim ("your nails record your heart")

---

## 4. Pattern Interrupts

- Direct command
- Unexpected apology
- Shocking statistic
- Threat during a safe activity ("while you slept, your brain lost oxygen")

---

## 5. Authority Hooks

1. Introduce credentials **after** tension, never before.
2. Pair the presenter ID with exactly one hard statistic from a named source.
3. Position the villain as "rushed doctors" or "the industry," never the viewer.

---

## 6. Story Hooks

Cold-open with a specific character (age, vivid positive detail), an ordinary trigger phrase, then a devastating reversal, then a hook line such as "that story has a name."

---

## 7. Retention Methods

1. Delay the greeting until after tension is established.
2. Use short, punchy lines.
3. Deploy a symptom checklist for self-recognition.
4. Re-open a loop every 60–90 seconds.
5. Restate the strongest payoff near the end.

---

## 8. Hook Rules

| ID | Rule | Severity |
|----|------|----------|
| HR1 | Credentials appear after tension | Block |
| HR2 | Exactly one hard statistic | Warn |
| HR3 | Villain is the system, not the viewer | Warn |
| HR4 | End the 60s on an open loop | Block |

---

## 9. Failure Modes to Avoid

- Soft greetings before tension ("Good evening…" with no hook)
- Vague statistics ("respected institutions")
- Benefit stated without a curiosity gap
