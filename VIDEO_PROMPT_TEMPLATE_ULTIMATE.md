## 🚨 CRITICAL NEW FEATURE: NARRATIVE CONTEXT SYSTEM

### THE BREAKTHROUGH DISCOVERY

**THE PROBLEM WE SOLVED:**
When script lines are extracted in isolation (25-27 words), AI doesn't understand what's actually happening in the story. This causes mismatched visuals.

**Example of the Problem:**
- Script Line: "She realizes in June that she's being prepared for a future, not punished for a past."
- **WITHOUT Narrative Context:** AI generates generic "woman thinking about future vs past" imagery
- **WITH Narrative Context:** AI generates "woman doing farm work having realization about rehabilitation training"

**THE SOLUTION:**
Every prompt now has THREE sections:
1. **Script Line** - Exact words from original script (for editor/voiceover sync)
2. **Narrative Context** - What's ACTUALLY happening in the story (for AI understanding)
3. **AI Image Prompt** - Visual description built from narrative context (for Midjourney)

## 🎯 NARRATIVE CONTEXT WRITING RULES

**MANDATORY REQUIREMENTS:**

### 1. READ FULL SCRIPT FIRST
Before generating ANY prompts, read the ENTIRE original script to understand:
- Complete story arc
- All characters and their roles
- Key locations and settings
- Emotional journey
- Important themes and motifs

### 2. NARRATIVE CONTEXT QUALITY STANDARDS

**Each narrative context MUST include:**

✅ **Location/Setting:** Where is this scene happening?
- Example: "Interior of British POW camp mess hall" NOT just "a room"

✅ **Characters Present:** Who is in this scene?
- Example: "Margarethe and British cook" NOT just "people"

✅ **Actions Occurring:** What is physically happening?
- Example: "Cook refilling prisoner's bowl without being asked" NOT just "serving food"

✅ **Emotional/Story Significance:** Why does this moment matter?
- Example: "First moment of unexpected kindness breaking propaganda beliefs" NOT just "nice gesture"

✅ **Context for Isolated Words:** Explain terms that lack meaning without context
- If script says "She realizes in June..." explain what she realizes and why

**Length:** 2-4 clear sentences (approximately 40-80 words)

**Tone:** Descriptive, specific, story-focused (like a director explaining a scene to a cinematographer)

### 3. NARRATIVE CONTEXT EXAMPLES

**❌ BAD NARRATIVE CONTEXT (too vague):**
"Woman thinking about something important while working."

**✅ GOOD NARRATIVE CONTEXT (specific and clear):**
"Margarethe is doing farm work at Mr. Hewitt's farm in June 1946. While learning to repair farm equipment, she has a realization that the British are teaching her practical skills for post-war German reconstruction, not punishing her. This is a moment of understanding that her labor has forward-looking purpose."

**❌ BAD NARRATIVE CONTEXT (missing story context):**
"Someone getting medical treatment for an injury."

**✅ GOOD NARRATIVE CONTEXT (includes story meaning):**
"Margarethe has cut her hand on barbed wire and expects to be dismissed or told to keep working, based on her experience with German military medical attitudes. Instead, the British corporal immediately escorts her to the medical clinic where a British nurse treats her wound with genuine care, including apologizing during the suturing process."

**MANDATORY RULES:**

6. ✅ **STOP IMMEDIATELY when original script ends**
   - Do NOT generate prompts beyond the original script
   - Do NOT invent or hallucinate additional script lines
   - Do NOT create "conclusion" prompts unless in original script

## 🛑 SCRIPT-END DETECTION SYSTEM (NEW)

### MANDATORY STOP PROTOCOL

**BEFORE generating each batch of prompts:**

1. **Calculate Remaining Script:**
   - Track exact word position in original script
   - Calculate: Total Script Words - Words Already Extracted = Remaining Words
   - Estimate prompts remaining: Remaining Words ÷ 26 = Approximate Prompts Left

2. **Pre-Batch Verification:**
   - Before starting each 50-prompt batch, verify script continues
   - If remaining script < 50 prompts worth, generate only remaining amount
   - Never assume script continues - always verify

3. **Stop Conditions - HALT IMMEDIATELY if:**
   - ✅ Reached the last sentence of original script
   - ✅ No more words remain in original script
   - ✅ Next extraction would go beyond script end

4. **When Stopping:**
   - State final prompt number clearly
   - Report total words extracted
   - Confirm original script is 100% complete
   - Do NOT add "conclusion" or "outro" unless in original script

**Example Stop Message:**
```

## 🚫 STRICT CONSTRAINTS

### What AI Image Generators CANNOT Do:
- ❌ Generate legible text, numbers, or words
- ❌ Create specific real people's faces (celebrities, politicians)
- ❌ Generate logos or branded content accurately
- ❌ Create precise data visualizations with exact numbers
- ❌ Generate copyrighted characters or IP

### Working Around Limitations:
- ✅ For text needs: Use "book with text visible" or "screen showing data" (text will be gibberish but looks real)
- ✅ For data viz: Use "infographic style chart" or "graph showing trends" (won't have real numbers but looks professional)
- ✅ For people: Use "professional person" or "diverse team" (generic faces, not specific individuals)
- ✅ For logos: Use "corporate branding elements" or "professional badge" (abstract professional looking imagery)

## 🎯 SYNCHRONIZATION RULES

### Semantic Matching Priority
**The visual must match the MEANING of the script, not just the words:**

1. **Identify the KEY CONCEPT** in each script chunk (via narrative context)
   - What is the main subject?
   - What is the main action or idea?
   - What is the emotional tone?

2. **Match to Visual Vocabulary**
   - If narrative describes growth → Use growth imagery
   - If narrative describes security → Use protection imagery
   - If narrative describes innovation → Use breakthrough imagery

3. **Consider Narrative Context** (this is the breakthrough)
   - Problem being discussed in narrative? → Show challenge/obstacle visuals
   - Solution being presented in narrative? → Show resolution/clarity visuals
   - Transition happening in narrative? → Show transformation/change visuals

4. **Timing Consideration**
   - Visual appears on screen for entire 8 seconds
   - Visual matches the 25-27 words being spoken
   - Transition happens at natural speech pauses/sentence breaks

**STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS:**

1. **Read the Narrative Context first**
   - Understand what's actually happening in the story

   - Characters → Who to show

   - Mood → How it should feel

3. **Build Prompt Structure:**
   ```
