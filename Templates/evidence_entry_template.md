## Safety Review

- Does the source support the exact claim?
- Is the claim stronger than the evidence?
- Are all limitations recorded?
- Are medication, dosing, serious disease, reversal, or contraindication caveats required?
- Does the wording avoid cure, guarantee, diagnosis, or medication alteration?
